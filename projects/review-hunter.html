Review Hunter System

A Google Sheets workspace with a sidebar that turns slow, inconsistent review handling into a fast, tracked workflow.

  • RoleDesign & build, from scratch
  • TypeInternal operational tool
  • StackApps Script · HTML/CSS/JS · AI-assisted
The problem

Handled slowly, and inconsistently.

Customer feedback that needed a response went through a manual loop: read it, decide what to do, write a response, log the outcome. There was no single place to see which cases had been handled and which were still waiting.

The process wasn't broken in one obvious place. It was eroded everywhere, a little: no queue, no consistent format, no record.

The approach

One workspace, three moves.

The system is a Google Sheets workspace with a custom sidebar. The sheet holds the state; the sidebar does the work.

  • Fetch. The latest feedback is pulled into a work queue automatically.
  • Draft. Each item gets a response grounded in what the customer actually wrote.
  • Track. Every case carries a status, so handling is visible at a glance.

It runs where the team already works, inside Sheets. No new apps, no new logins, no training session required.

The build

How it was built

  • A custom sidebar interface inside Google Sheets, written in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Automated retrieval that feeds a structured work queue.
  • Response drafting that uses the content of each review.
  • Status tracking with a visible record of every case.
  • Everything on Google Apps Script: zero infrastructure, zero new tools.
The outcome
What changed

Reviews get handled faster and consistently, with a record of every one. The queue is visible, the responses are grounded in the feedback, and nothing waits silently.
